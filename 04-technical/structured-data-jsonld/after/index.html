How to cold-brew coffee at home

By Marta Ibáñez · Published September 1, 2026 · Zentimes Kitchen

Cold brew is coffee steeped in cold water for many hours instead of being extracted with heat. The result is a smooth, low-acid concentrate you dilute to taste. This guide uses a 1:8 coffee-to-water ratio and a 12-hour steep at room temperature.

Ingredients

  • 100 g coarsely ground coffee
  • 800 ml filtered water, cold

Steps

  1. Add the coarse grounds to a large jar.
  2. Pour in the cold water and stir until fully saturated.
  3. Cover and steep for 12 hours at room temperature.
  4. Strain through a paper filter into a clean bottle.
  5. Dilute the concentrate 1:1 with water or milk and serve over ice.

Total time is about 12 hours and 10 minutes, almost all of it hands-off steeping. The sealed concentrate keeps in the fridge for up to two weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use pre-ground coffee?

Yes, but a coarse grind is best. Fine grounds slip through the filter and make the brew cloudy and over-extracted.

How long does cold brew last?

The undiluted concentrate keeps for up to 14 days in a sealed bottle in the refrigerator.
